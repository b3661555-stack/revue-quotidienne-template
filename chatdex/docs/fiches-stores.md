## English

**Name:** Chatdex: Catch 'em Cats
**Subtitle (App Store):** Collect the cats you meet
**Short description (Play):** Photograph the cats you meet, collect them and see who else found them.
**Keywords (App Store):** cat,cats,kitten,collection,photo,street cats,game,walk,explore,map,community,hunt

**Description:**
Every cat you meet can join your collection.

See a cat on your walk? Snap it. Chatdex checks whether another hunter already found it. If it's new, it's your 👑 First Catch. If not, you become its 8th, 12th or 50th hunter, and its story grows.

• Build your Chatdex: every cat gets its own profile with photos, looks, personality tags and sightings.
• Explore the map: cats around you, weekly hotspots and hunters nearby.
• Rarity from Common to Legendary, plus rare Shiny cats.
• Earn XP, level up and unlock 15 badges (Night Hunter, Explorer, Cat Detective…).
• Follow friends, react to their finds and compare collections.
• Start a Cat Hunt: a timed walk with friends to find as many cats as possible.
• A cat nobody has seen for a month? Find it again and the whole community is notified 🚨

Respect first: locations are always approximate (never a home), and the app reminds you to watch from public ground, never chase, pick up or feed cats that aren't yours.

Available in 15 languages. No ads, no tracking.
